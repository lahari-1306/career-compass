About CareerCompass

FIND YOUR TRUE NORTH

CareerCompass was created with a clear, uncompromising mission: to provide Indian students with reliable, trustworthy, and actionable education and career navigation.

Indian students completing Class 10, Intermediate, Polytechnic Diploma, or Undergraduate degrees often face fragmented, outdated, and conflicting information across social media and unregulated blogs. CareerCompass consolidates authentic options across academics, competitive examinations, public sector opportunities, defence entries, and legal study resources.

Our Core Guarantees:

  • Zero Hallucination: We never invent exam dates, cutoffs, eligibility criteria, or official links.
  • Active Timestamps: Our notification engine dynamically compares current dates against verified windows, automatically discarding expired notices.
  • Official Source Accountability: Every career card, exam notice, and scholarship links directly to the legal government portal.
  • Dual AI Intelligence: Server-side Google Gemini integration when active, paired with an offline rule-based knowledge engine that never crashes.
